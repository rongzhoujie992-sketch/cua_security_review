# TDES methods note

The targeted deployment-evidence supplement covers identity and credential configuration, execution isolation, human oversight, operational constraints, supply-chain exposure, and post-incident response.

The academic route re-extracted deployment fields from eight studies already in the main CUA corpus and assessed sixteen additional academic candidates: fifteen targeted-search records and one seed source. Four supplementary academic sources were retained for nonredundant source-native evidence on the defined deployment conditions.

The official-document route reviewed twelve provider families and twenty-three official deployment-document locators. Five documents from Microsoft, AWS, and Anthropic were retained because they document distinct deployment mechanisms.

The academic and official-document branches are supplementary evidence routes and are not included in the 222-study main corpus.

# TDES methods note

To address RQ4, a targeted deployment-evidence supplement was conducted for identity and credential configuration, execution isolation, human oversight, operational constraints, supply-chain exposure, and post-incident response.

The academic route revisited eight studies already represented in the final main CUA corpus and assessed sixteen additional academic candidates, comprising fifteen targeted-search records and one additional seed source. Four supplementary academic sources were selected because they directly addressed the deployment conditions synthesized in Section 5 and contributed non-redundant source-native evidence.

Separately, twelve provider families and twenty-three official deployment-document locators were reviewed. Five official deployment documents from Microsoft, AWS, and Anthropic were selected because they directly instantiated deployment mechanisms synthesized in Section 5.

The academic-source and official-document branches are supplementary evidence and are not counted in the 222-study main corpus.

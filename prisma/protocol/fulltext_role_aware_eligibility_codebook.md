# Role-aware full-text eligibility criteria

A study is eligible for the main review corpus only if it satisfies at least one of the following roles.

1. **Direct security** — direct digital CUA/GUI/OS/Web/mobile/browser security, privacy, attack, defense, unsafe-operation, or security-evaluation evidence.
2. **Adjacent mechanism** — a mechanism that bears directly on executable computer use, especially tool/MCP/skill execution, permission/authorization/provenance, persistent memory/state, or agent-to-agent communication/delegation that materially affects execution or shared state.
3. **Necessary contextual premise** — a limited, non-substitutable environment, interface, action-space, benchmark, long-horizon/state, evaluator, or taxonomy source actually required by the synthesis.

## Eligibility rules

- Relevance alone is insufficient.
- Capability/background papers are retained only when the premise is necessary rather than merely useful.
- General LLM/agent security is excluded when the executable-computer-use linkage is indirect or not used by the synthesis.
- Preprint/under-review work faces a higher evidence threshold and must add a distinct, technically substantive contribution.
- Near-redundant benchmarks/frameworks are not automatically retained merely because they match topic keywords.
- Report-version reconciliation acts on reports, not studies.
- Inclusion in the review does not imply eligibility for every security synthesis; claim boundaries remain role-specific.

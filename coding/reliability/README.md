# Reliability and Adjudication

The primary coder and adjudicator was Zhoujie RONG (Rong Zhoujie), the manuscript author. The independent second coder was Fang Jingran (Jingran Fang).

An independent second coder completed a blinded operation-level coding exercise over a stratified 20% sample of the corpus under the frozen HPAT codebook. Candidate identification, HPAT eligibility, and comparable relation fields were independently assessed and subsequently adjudicated. The complete 61-unit operation ledger is `hpat_operation_adjudication_records.csv`.

Candidate IDs are coder-local, so paired candidate metrics remain restricted to the 22 source-native one-to-one matches approved during unitization review. Partial, split/merge, and unmatched unitizations are documented and adjudicated in the operation ledger rather than treated as same-unit agreement.

The independent contribution-role exercise contains 54 matched study-by-role labels across 18 studies. Labels were compared and disagreements were adjudicated under the frozen role contract. These quantities describe the stated paired samples and are not extrapolated to the complete corpus.
